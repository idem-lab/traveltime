# traveltime: Calculate Travel Times Over Space

Calculate travel time across a landscape from a set of user-specified
locations, over a friction surface describing the time taken to cross
each cell. Implements the least-cost travel time approach of Weiss et
al. (2018)
[doi:10.1038/nature25181](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature25181) and Weiss
et al. (2020)
[doi:10.1038/s41591-020-1059-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-020-1059-1)
, and provides convenient access to their global walking and motorised
friction surfaces via the 'malariaAtlas' package. Travel times are
computed with 'terra'. See Ryan et al. (2025)
[doi:10.12688/gatesopenres.16356.1](https://doi.org/10.12688/gatesopenres.16356.1)
for further details.
